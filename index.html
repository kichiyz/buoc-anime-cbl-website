Concern & Inquiry Desk

Have a question, concern, complaint, or suggestion? Send it to BUOC through this private form. An authorized officer can review it, reply through the website, and update its status. Please do not submit passwords, government ID numbers, or unnecessary sensitive personal information.

10–8,000 characters. Be respectful and avoid including private information about other people unless necessary.
