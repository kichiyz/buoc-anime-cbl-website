                        CONSTITUTION AND BY LAWS


                                          Article I

                                   NAME AND NATURE

Section 1.

This organization shall be officially known as the Bicol University Otaku Circle.
Hereinafter, the organization shall be referred to as the BU Otaku Circle or BUOC.

Section 2.

“Otaku” is a Japanese term referring to “geek” or “nerd” but it is typically used to refer to
a fan of anime, video games or Japanese Culture in general. Thus, Bicol University
Otaku Circle is a University Based Organization established by Otaku students of Bicol
University.

Section 3.

The Bicol University Otaku Circle caters to those who are anime and cosplay
enthusiasts, photographers and videographers, traditional and digital artists, online and
offline gamers, and everything else in the spectrum; hence the name Otaku Circle.



                                          Article II

                                       OBJECTIVES

Section 1.

General Objective: “To promote Otaku and Japanese Culture to the students of Bicol
University, and to be able to flourish the extra-curricular engagement of the students.”

Section 2.

Specific Objectives:

To bridge the academic excellence of Bicol University to the students.
To foster unity and camaraderie among members of Otaku Circle.

To serve as a training ground for its members to become academically and socially
competitive.

To be able to provide a creative and technical platform to enhance a student’s creativity
and establish relations with other students that have similar advocacies and interests.



                                          Article III

                  DECLARATION OF ORGANIZATION’S PRINCIPLES

Section 1.

The organization encourages nationalism and youth involvement on public and civic
affairs.

Section 2.

The organization shall maintain honesty and integrity in the public service.

Section 3.

The organization shall pursue non-partisan objectives, programs, and undertakings.

Section 4.

The organization believes that an Otaku BUeño is capable of excellence in various
fields.



                                         Article IV

                                       MEMBERSHIP

Section 1.

The Bicol University Otaku Circle is a University Based Organization whose members
are bona-fide students of the Bicol University (Undergraduates and Graduate School).

Section 2.

Nature of Application:

Application is voluntary and shall be open every academic year.

Applicants must be officially enrolled in Bicol University.
Application period shall run for the first and second semester of the academic year,
applicants are required to attend all activities of the organization and provide a
membership fee amounting to Php 100.00 for the whole academic year consisting of
Php 50.00 per semester with the following benefit: An official BUOC enamel membership
pin, provided that there are sufficient funds to support production and distribution.

All students from Bicol University are qualified to join upon the completion of the
application requirements prescribed by the organization.

Applicants shall be evaluated according to the performance and depending on the
criteria set by the PR-HR Committee.

The PR-HR Committee, together with the Executive Committee, shall decide on the final
list of applicants qualified for induction.

Section 3.

There shall be five standing committees: The Secretariat Committee, the Finance
Committee, the Planning Committee, the PR-HR Committee and the Ways and Means
Committee.

Section 4.

The PR-HR Committee shall distribute off members to their respective committees at the
start of every academic year based on:

For old members:

Member’s committee preference.

Participation and fulfillment of the member’s duties as stated in Article V, Section 2 of
this constitution, in the previous academic year.

For new members:

Member’s committee preference.

Performance in the approaches reflected by the scores accumulated in the course of the
application period.

Section 5.

Renewal of membership shall be held annually.

Section 6.

The following membership statuses are herein adapted:

Active Members shall be those that satisfy Sections 1 and 3 of these articles and
continuously perform their duties. They have full right as in Article V.
Inactive Members shall be those who satisfy the sections 1 and 3 of these articles but for
certain reasons, clearly justified before the PR-HR and Executive Committees, cannot
participate in at least 60% of the total activities of the organization.

Probationary Members shall be those who fail to attend the Final Rites and Induction but
otherwise satisfy the requirements set by the PR-HR Committee during the application
period.

Honorary Members shall be those who shall be conferred with this honor by the General
Assembly on the account of their significant contributions and services to the
organization.

Alumni Members shall be members of the organization who have graduated.



                                           Article V

                           RIGHTS AND DUTIES OF MEMBERS

Section 1.

The members of the organization shall have the following rights:

The right to co-ordinate activities or participate in the activities of the organization.

The right to use any of the organization’s facilities and materials, provided that the
president gave the consent.

The right to choose a committee and actively participate in it.

The right to vote and to be voted upon.

The right to run for office provided that he/she is qualified as stated in Article VII of this
constitution.

The right to recommend suspension or impeachment of a member or officer.

The right to speak out and be heard in the General Assembly.

The right to suggest projects or any other necessary actions that would help the
improvement of the organization.

The right to be formally notified, heard, and adjudicated by the General Assembly before
being suspended or expelled.

The right to defend his/her actions when in question.

Section 2.
The members of the organization shall have the following duties:

Abide by and uphold the Bicol University Otaku Circle Constitution.

Know his/her rights and duties as a member and understand the principles of the
organization.

Fulfill all duties that may be entrusted to him/her by the organization.

Maintain peace and order within the organization, and refrain from any action that may
be detrimental to the organization.

Participate in at least 60% of the activities of the organization.

Can be a volunteer to one committee per academic year.

Dutifully participate and attend the meetings of his/her committee.

Maintain and improve the facilities of the organization.

Respect all decisions of the organization that are agreed upon in the General Assembly.

Accomplish his/her financial obligations in time.

Attend at least 60% of all the General Assemblies of the organization, except for
members who can present a valid reason before the Executive Committee.



                                          Article VI

                            ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE

Section 1.

There shall be a General Assembly composed of all members of the organization. The
assembly shall determine all policies and decisions of the organization, except for those
which had been already specified.

Section 2.

There shall be an Executive Committee composed of the President/Chairperson, Internal
Vice President, External Vice President, Secretary General, Deputy Secretary General,
Finance General, Deputy Finance General, Auditor, The Business Managers, Public
Information Officer, Chief Campus Representatives, and Deputy Campus
Representatives.

Section 3.
There shall be five standing committees: The Secretariat Committee, the Finance
Committee, the Planning Committee, the PR-HR Committee and the Ways and Means
Committee.

Section 4.

Ad Hoc Committees shall be created by the Executive Committee and shall perform
special functions not covered by the committees mentioned in Section 3 of this Article.
However, once the function of the Ad Hoc Committee is done, the committee shall be
dissolved.



                                        Article VII

                           QUALIFICATIONS FOR OFFICERS

Section 1.

All active members are qualified to run for office in the organization provided that they
satisfy Article IV, Sections 1 and 3.

Section 2.

The President shall:

Have at least three (3) consecutive semesters of active membership in the organization
prior to his/her term.

Can hold top position in any other organization, whether university-based or college-
based, during his/her term providing he/she will do his/her obligations and
responsibilities in the organization prior to his/her term.

Not have been subjected to any disciplinary action by the university.

Section 3.

The Vice-Presidents shall have at least two (2) consecutive semesters of active
membership in the organization prior to their term and shall not have been subjected to
any disciplinary action by the university.

Section 4.

All other officers shall be active members of the organization and shall not have been
subjected to any disciplinary action by the university.



                                        Article VIII
                            OFFICER’S RESPONSIBILITIES

Section 1.

The responsibilities of the President/Chairperson are as follows:

He/she is the head of the Executive Committee.

He/She shall approve, facilitate and supervise meetings, projects and activities of the
organization.

He/she shall resolve conflicts regarding the Executive Committee.

He/She shall represent and bear the organization’s name in formal events, meetings and
activities.

He/She shall carry out and implement policies and constitution amendments approved
by the General Assembly.

He/She shall take charge with the over-all supervision of the standing committees.

He/She shall create Ad hoc committee in cases it is deemed necessary.

Section 2.

The responsibilities of the Internal Vice President are as follows:

He/she shall serve as the Officer-In-Charge in case the President is not available.

He/she shall facilitate internal activities like acquaintance parties and other bonding
activities of the organization.

He/she shall be a co-head of the Planning Committee.

He/she shall assist the President in all matters where his presence is required.

Section 3.

The responsibilities of the External Vice President are as follows:

He/she shall serve as the Officer-In-Charge in case the President is not available.

He/she shall supervise all external activities like council meetings of the University, and
other related activities.

He/She shall be a co-head of the Planning Committee.

He/she shall represent and bear the organization’s name in formal events, meetings and
activities in cases that the President is not available.

Section 4.
The responsibilities of the Secretary General are as follows:

He/she is the head of the Secretariat Committee.

He/she shall handle all records and files of the organization.

He/she shall prepare all the Accomplishment Reports of the organization and present
them at the end of the semester.

He/she shall evaluate project proposals planned by the organization.

He/she shall record and keep the minutes of every meeting.

Section 5.

The responsibilities of the Deputy Secretary General are as follows:

He/she is the co-head of the Secretariat Committee.

He/she shall help the Secretary General handle all records and files of the organization.

He/she shall help the Secretary General prepare all the Accomplishment Reports of the
organization and present them at the end of the semester.

He/she shall help the Secretary General evaluate project proposals planned by the
organization.

Section 6.

The responsibilities of the Finance General are as follows:

He/she is the head of the Finance Committee.

He/she shall prepare all the Finance Reports of the organization and present them at the
end of the semester.

He/she shall evaluate budgetary proposals planned by the organization.

He/she shall act as the custodian of funds of the Organization.

Section 7.

The responsibilities of the Deputy Finance General are as follows:

He/she is the co-head of the Finance Committee.

He/she shall help the finance officer prepare all the Finance Reports of the organization
and present them at the end of the semester.

He/she shall help the Finance officer evaluate budgetary proposals planned by the
organization.
Section 8.

The Responsibilities of the Auditor are as follows:

He/she shall ensure the compliance with established internal control procedures by
examining records, reports, operating practices and documentation.

He/she shall audit the financial and accomplishment reports provided by the secretariat
and finance committee.

Section 9.

The Responsibilities of the Business Managers are as follows:

They shall allocate resources to accomplish desired goals and objectives efficiently and
effectively.

They shall be the heads of the Ways and Means Committee.

They shall act as property custodian give consent to such member(s) utilization of any of
the organization’s facilities and materials.

Section 10.

The responsibilities of the Public Information Officer are as follows:

He/she shall be the head of the PR-HR committee.

He/she shall plan publicity strategies and campaigns, and deal with the inquiries of the
public.

He/she shall serve as the official spokesperson in behalf of the organization.

Section 11.

The responsibilities of the Chief Campus Representatives and Deputy Campus
Representatives are as follows:

They shall represent the organization in their respective campuses (Main, Daraga, East).

They shall assist the Executive Committee in coordinating and implementing campus-
specific activities.



                                         Article IX

                                STANDING COMMITTEES

Section 1.
The Secretariat Committee shall have the following functions:

Assist the Secretary General and the Deputy Secretary General perform his/her duties.

Take charge of the documents and records of the organization.

Help disseminate information to all other members of the organization.

The Secretary General shall be the head of the Secretariat Committee, and the Deputy
Secretary General being the co-head.

Section 2.

The Finance Committee shall have the following functions:

Be responsible for the organization’s finance.

Initiate fund-raising projects for the organization.

Collect dues and fees from the applicants and members of the organization.

See to it that the organization’s finances are used for the welfare and benefit of the
members and the organization itself.

The Finance General shall be the head of the Finance Committee, and the Deputy
Finance General being the co-head.

Section 3.

The PR-HR Committee shall have the following functions:

Be in charge of the application process.

Together with the Executive Committee evaluate the status of members every semester
and decide on probation cases.

Screen and evaluate applicants for membership along with the Executive Committee.

Supervise the reaffirmation process of the members.

Initiate activities that foster camaraderie, cooperation, solidarity, and unity among the
applicants and members.

Formulate and implement application guidelines, requirements, procedures, provided
that the Executive Committee approves.

Help disseminate information to all other members of the organization.

Secure and store a copy of the constitution and by-laws.

The Public Information Officer shall be the head of the PR-HR Committee.
Section 4.

The Ways and Means Committee shall have the following functions:

To use funds entrusted to the club for such purposes as will.

Be concerned with tall matters associated with raising the organization’s funds.

The Business Managers shall be the heads of the Ways and Means Committee.

Section 5.

The Planning Committee shall have the following functions:

To assist in the formulation, supervising, and execution of plans, projects and other
endeavors of the organization.

To work alongside with the Secretariat and Executive Committee in the creation and
preparation of the mandatory foundations of events and activities that shall be facilitated
by the organization.

The External Vice President and the Internal Vice President shall be the co-heads of the
Planning Committee.



                                         Article X

                                 GENERAL ASSEMBLY

Section 1.

The General Assembly is the highest policy making body of the organization and shall
have the final authority in any activities of the organization.

Section 2.

The General Assembly shall be composed of all members of the organization.

Section 3.

The General Assembly shall have the following powers:

Elect the members of the Executive Committee.

Exercise the final authority on matters affecting the organization.

Amend the Constitution and other policies upon approval of the majority.

Take the decision on the expulsion of a member.
Suspend or impeach any member of the Executive Committee, upon the approval of
75% of the voting body.

Section 4.

The General Assembly shall be held twice in every semester.



                                         Article XI

                                       ELECTIONS

Section 1.

Nominations for officers shall be held during the first general assembly of the
organization facilitated by the incumbent officers.

Section 2.

The schedule for the election of new officers and the first general assembly shall be set
by the Executive Committee.

Section 3.

The nomination and election of officers shall be supervised and facilitated by the
Executive Committee.

Section 4.

Election of officers shall be conducted through casting of votes.

Section 5.

Only active members are allowed to nominate and vote.

Section 6.

Incumbent officers may be reelected once for the same office.

Section 7.

All elected officers shall have tenure of one (1) academic year.

Section 8.

The new set of duly elected officers shall resume office a day after the election on the
first semester of the academic year and will end at the start of the first semester of the
following academic year.

Section 9.
A candidate is proclaimed winner if he/she gets at least fifty percent plus one (50% + 1)
of the votes cast. If no candidate satisfies the condition, the top two (2) candidates shall
vile for the position again.

Section 10.

The Executive Committee shall do a temporary appointment immediately, on case of
permanent vacation of an elected officer.



                                          Article XII

                                  LOSS OF MEMBERSHIP

Section 1.

Expulsion is the permanent loss of membership from the organization. Expelled
members cannot be readmitted to the organization.

Section 2.

The following are grounds for expulsion:

Incurrence of three (3) or more violations or offenses within a semester, as stated in
Section 3 of this Article.

Non-attendance in all General Assemblies.

Gross violation of the Constitution.

Commissions on acts highly detrimental to the interest, integrity, and well-being of the
organization.

Section 3.

Each of the following shall be considered a violation:

Three (3) unexcused consecutive absences from the General Assembly.

Non-participation in at least 60% of all the activities of the organization.

Unreasonable neglect of duty entrusted by the organization.

Commission of acts detrimental to the interest, integrity, and well-being of the
organization.

Non-payment of financial obligations on time.

Destruction of the organization’s facilities.
Section 4.

Any member shall lose his/her membership if he/she fails to renew or if he/she
withdraws his/her membership.



                                       Article XIII

                                  FACULTY ADVISER

Section 1.

The organization shall have one (1) Faculty Adviser duly elected every year by the active
members.

Section 2.

The Faculty Adviser shall advice and guide the organization regarding the activities.

Section 3.

There is no limit to the number of times the Faculty Adviser may be chosen.



                                       Article XIV

                                  NO HAZING POLICY

Section 1.

The organization strongly opposes hazing or any violent acts that may endanger the
lives of the applicants during the application process.

Section 2.

Those who will violate this act shall automatically be terminated from the organization.



                                        Article XV

                       NO USING OF ILLEGAL DRUGS POLICY

Section 1.

The organization strongly opposes the use of illegal drugs that may endanger the lives of
its members and applicants.

Section 2.
Those who will violate this act shall be automatically terminated from the organization.



                                        Article XVI

                                      AMENDMENTS

Section 1.

Amendments of this Constitution may be proposed by a member, provided it is first
endorsed in writing by at least five (5) active members to the Executive Committee not
later than three (3) days before the General Assembly wherein it will be formally
presented. Those members proposing the amendment are charged with the
responsibility of making sure that all active members are aware of the content of the
proposed amendment.

Section 2.

The amendment shall be adapted upon arrival of two- thirds (2/3) of all the active
members present during the said General Assembly. No amendment shall be acted
upon without prior notice to all active members.

We, the members of this organization, do hereby swear unto this constitution. With our
policies and principles, this constitution is a manifestation of our primary objectives and
purpose. This Constitution is presented for the members of the organization to accept
and abide by, so hereby promulgated:
